## Rhetorical line options

### “I don't guess.”

1. **Δεν μαντεύω.** Selected. Very short and direct; preserves the blunt force of the English.
2. **Δεν κάνω υποθέσεις.** Sounds slightly more analytical and closer to research language, but loses some of the sharpness.
3. **Δεν βασίζομαι σε εικασίες.** More explicit and professional, but longer and a little more formal than the source.

### “I like the product work that lives in the details…”

1. **Μου αρέσει η δουλειά στο product που κρίνεται στις λεπτομέρειες: ξεκάθαρα flows, συνεπή patterns και λύσεις που οι ομάδες μπορούν πράγματι να υλοποιήσουν.** Selected. “Κρίνεται” makes the details consequential and sounds natural in a Greek product team.
2. **Μου αρέσει το product work που βρίσκεται στις λεπτομέρειες: καθαρά flows, συνεπή patterns και λύσεις που οι ομάδες μπορούν όντως να χτίσουν.** Closer to the English image, but the opening is more heavily Anglicised.
3. **Στο product, μου αρέσει η δουλειά της λεπτομέρειας: καθαρά flows, συνέπεια στα patterns και λύσεις που μπορούν πράγματι να υλοποιηθούν από την ομάδα.** More idiomatic and reflective, but less terse and less active.

## Translation notes and uncertainties

- **UX research & product:** The English label was changed from "UX practitioner" on 4 Aug 2026, partly because both translations struggled with it. The earlier Greek rendering **Επαγγελματίας UX** was replaced: it reads as "UX professional", which implies professional UX experience and breaks the project's no-overclaiming rule. Greek practitioners keep this terminology in English, so the label stays **UX research & product** untranslated.
- **Agent X:** I kept **φοιτητικό project**, **με καθοδήγηση καθηγητών**, **ο πρώτος ολοκληρωμένος κύκλος** and **ένας ολοκληρωμένος κύκλος** explicit. I avoided stronger words such as “ολοκληρωμένη μελέτη”, “client project” or “case study” in its description.
- **Radio:** I kept **προσωπικό project** and **χωρίς να γράψω τον κώδικα** explicit. “Καθοδήγηση” is intentionally weaker than language that would imply engineering ownership.
- **Figma:** **σε βασικό επίπεδο** and the limitation that follows are retained without softening.
- **“Could carry a newcomer through”:** I chose **θα μπορούσε να βοηθήσει έναν νέο παίκτη να προχωρήσει**, weaker and more natural than wording that would claim the guidance successfully “onboards” or “guides” the player.
- **“Taught me”:** I kept **με έμαθαν** rather than “μου έδωσαν expertise”, which would overstate the claim.
- **“Auditing”:** Depending on the team, Greek practitioners may also say *audit*. I used **έλεγχος** because it is natural and does not imply a formal certified audit.
- **Numbers:** Counts and year ranges are preserved. I rendered **SEK 1.5B+** exactly in the requested local form, **1,5 δισ. SEK**; the following caveat still makes clear that this was an estimate, not spend or revenue.
- **“All regions”:** **Όλες οι περιφέρειες** is the closest compact Greek rendering of Sweden’s regional authorities. It does not imply geographic operations beyond the framework coverage stated underneath.
- **Language levels:** I used **Άπταιστα** for “Fluent”, avoiding **Άριστα**, which could imply a stronger or formally assessed level.
- **Mixed Greek and English:** I retained the English terms Greek practitioners commonly use aloud—AI, UX, wireframes, personas, journey map, stakeholders, product management and related product/build terms. Less established terms were translated where a plain Greek equivalent was more natural.
