## Rhetorical line options

### “I don't guess.”

1. **Jag gissar inte.** Selected. Short, blunt and closest to the force of the English.
2. **Jag utgår inte från antaganden.** More analytical and explicitly research-oriented, but less sharp.
3. **Jag chansar inte.** More colloquial and suggests avoiding careless risk; stronger and slightly different from the source.

### “I like the product work that lives in the details…”

1. **Det produktarbete jag gillar finns i detaljerna: tydliga flöden, konsekventa mönster och lösningar som team faktiskt kan bygga.** Selected. Natural Swedish and closest to the English image without sounding translated.
2. **Jag gillar produktarbetet som avgörs i detaljerna: tydliga flöden, konsekventa mönster och lösningar som team faktiskt kan bygga.** Gives the details more consequence, but “avgörs” is slightly stronger than “lives”.
3. **Jag gillar det detaljerade produktarbetet: tydliga flöden, konsekventa mönster och lösningar som team faktiskt kan bygga.** The most compact CV phrasing, but it loses the rhetorical contrast in “lives in the details”.

## Translation notes and uncertainties

- **UX research & product:** The English label was changed from "UX practitioner" on 4 Aug 2026, partly because both translations struggled with it. The earlier **UX-utövare** was replaced with **UX-research & produkt**, which names the field without claiming a title or a seniority level.
- **Projects:** The English label was **Work** until 5 Aug 2026. Swedish had already gone to **Projekt**, because both entries are projects and *Arbete* sounds unnatural as a Swedish CV heading, so the English caught up with the Swedish rather than the other way round. The translation is unchanged. This is a label, not a change of status: Agent X remains explicitly **Studentprojekt**.
- **Approach:** Previously **Practice**, translated **Arbetssätt**. The Swedish is unchanged, since *Arbetssätt* covers both the old and the new English label and is the natural Swedish CV heading for a section about how someone works. *Angreppssätt* was avoided as too clinical, and *Metod* would claim a more formal method than the section describes.
- **Agent X:** I retained **med lärarhandledning**, **min första fullständiga researchcykel**, **en komplett cykel** and the testing limitations. I avoided *kundprojekt*, *uppdrag* and wording that would imply independent professional delivery.
- **Radio:** I retained **Personligt projekt** and **utan att skriva koden**. **Styrning** reflects directing the build without implying that the applicant engineered it.
- **Figma:** **på grundläggande nivå** is intentionally plain, and the following sentence explicitly says it is not a claimed strength.
- **“Carry a newcomer through”:** I used **hjälpa en ny spelare att ta sig vidare**, which is natural and modest. Stronger words such as *onboarda* or *leda hela vägen* would overstate what was being investigated.
- **“Running a regulated service business”:** I used **med ansvar för** rather than a grander leadership formulation. The experience entry itself still accurately says **Grundade och ledde** where the English does.
- **“Audit”:** I used **granskning**, not *revision*, because *revision* would suggest a formal financial or certified audit.
- **Numbers:** Counts and year ranges are preserved. I rendered **SEK 1.5B+** exactly in the requested local form, **1,5 md SEK**; the adjacent caveat continues to state that this was an estimate, not spend or company revenue.
- **“All regions”:** **Alla regioner** refers to Sweden’s regional authorities and is qualified immediately by **täckning genom ramavtal i Sverige**.
- **“Present”:** I used the normal compact Swedish CV form **idag**. **Pågående** was avoided because it could describe the company rather than the applicant’s role.
- **Industry English:** I kept terms that are normally used in English in Swedish UX/product work where that sounded natural, including UX, wireframes, personas, journey map, research and brief. I used ordinary Swedish where the Swedish term is the normal spoken equivalent, such as *intressenter*, *användbarhetstestning* and *produktledning*.
